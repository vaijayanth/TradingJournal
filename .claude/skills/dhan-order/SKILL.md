# dhan-order - real-money after-market orders for the Dhan (ND) stocks, one group at a time

**Tool names.** The Dhan tools appear as `mcp__dhan__*_agent_tool` or `mcp__claude_ai_dhan_mcp__*_agent_tool` depending on how the connector is attached: they are the same tools, use whichever is connected (a skill text that names one prefix is not wrong if only the other is present). Load their schemas with ToolSearch if they are deferred.

This skill places REAL orders on the user's Dhan account through the Dhan MCP. Signals are computed after the close and orders go in as AMO (after-market) orders for the NEXT trading day (or, before 09:00, the coming open). It works in six **stages, in this order**:

| # | Group | Side | Builder | Order type |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | SWING-SHARE (dh252) EXITS | SELL | `build_order_ticket.py exit` | MARKET AMO |
| 2 | SWING-SHARE (dh252) ADD-ONS | BUY | `build_order_ticket.py addon` | LIMIT AMO |
| 3 | SWING-SHARE (dh252) ENTRIES | BUY | `build_order_ticket.py entry` | LIMIT AMO |
| 4 | INVESTMENT-SHARE EXITS | SELL | `build_share_ticket.py exit` | MARKET AMO |
| 5 | INVESTMENT-SHARE AVERAGING | BUY | `build_share_ticket.py add` | LIMIT AMO |
| 6 | INVESTMENT-SHARE ENTRIES | BUY | `build_share_ticket.py entry` | LIMIT AMO |

Each stage is listed, the user may exclude stocks, the user confirms, the orders are placed and VERIFIED, and only then does the next stage start. This one skill replaces `dhan-order-entry`, `dhan-order-exit`, `dhan-invest-order-entry` and `dhan-invest-order-exit` (all deleted) and is the ND twin of `zerodha-order`; ND and AZ share no code or data. Option super orders are `dhan-superorder`, not this skill.

## Naming (user, 2026-11-17)
Two kinds of stocks, never mixed in the words: **swing shares** = the dh252 52-week-high swing stocks (stages 1-3); **investment shares** = the 10 covered-call investment-portfolio stocks (stages 4-6). Always say "investment-share exits / averaging / entries" and "swing-share exits / add-ons / entries", never a bare "share exits".

## The rules these orders come from
**dh252 swing stocks** (stages 1-3)
- Entry: 52-week high (closing high) + Supertrend green, not held; size = the effective tier (base Rs1,000, +tiers, doubling milestone), floored to whole shares, minimum 1 (a stock priced above the tier costs more than the tier; the user is fine with that, no warning is raised). Add-on: P&L at -6% or worse, 1/10 of the open quantity (minimum 1), once every 7 days. Exit: Supertrend RED, the whole position, any profit or loss. Fixed capital base Rs20,00,000. InvITs / REITs are not in the universe. All of it is computed by the engine; this skill never recomputes or overrides it.

**Investment-portfolio shares** (stages 4-6; the covered-call stocks in `dhan-live/data/cc_config.json` `universe`: MCX, SHRIRAMFIN, CGPOWER, BSE, DIXON, HCLTECH, INFY, BANKINDIA, BPCL, CUMMINSIND; user, 2026-11-08)
- **Exit:** Supertrend RED AND the position in profit, the whole position. A red stock in loss is HELD, not sold. The profit made is reinvested: the next entry in the same stock is sized at this position's capital plus the profit, which is why stage 4 records the capital before it places (`invest_trades.py pending`).
- **Averaging (stage 5):** once a week per stock when the Supertrend is green and the loss is worse than -6% vs average cost; quantity = 1/10 of the holding, minimum 1.
- **Entry (stage 6):** the Supertrend flips red to green on a stock you do not hold; quantity = the last position's capital plus the profit on that same stock in the previous trade (from `invest_trades.py`), divided by the close, floored to whole shares, minimum 1. If a flipped stock has no previous trade recorded the rule gives no size: the script requires `--qty SYMBOL=N` from the user; ask for it, never default it.

**Exercised 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-16:** 20 AMO BUY LIMIT CNC orders went in with the payload below (each first rejected by the broken pre-trade funds check, then accepted with `preflight: false` after the user's explicit yes) and all showed PENDING in the order book. Dhan's `place` response says `TRANSIT`; confirm in `orderbook_agent_tool` `list` before telling the user it is accepted.

## Scope (hard limits)
- Stocks only. Never options, futures, ETFs, the option-hedge portfolio, or a stock the engine does not list. Stages 1-3 never touch the investment-portfolio shares and stages 4-6 never touch dh252 stocks. A discretionary or partial sell is out of scope: refuse and say why.
- Only what the builders list (`dhan-live/dhan_live_signals.json` for stages 1-3; `dhan-live/covered_call_signals.json` `shares` for stages 4-6). Quantities come from the script, never from the user or you (an exit sells the whole position; the script checks it equals the Dhan holdings quantity). A stock with no signal is refused.
- **Pending orders are never ordered twice.** The builders read `dhan-live/data/orders_snapshot.json` and skip any signal that already has a pending order on the same side (they print `skipped SYMBOL: already has a pending BUY order (id)`); `--include-ordered` lists them anyway, only if the user asks. So the snapshot must be current: refresh it first (see Before the stages).
- **BUYs (add-ons, averaging, entries):** AMO LIMIT, CNC. **SELLs (exits, investment-share exits):** AMO MARKET, CNC: when an exit signal exists the position is sold at the next open whatever the price (the user's rule; no price guard blocks it). Never a market BUY. Never during market hours.
- **One group at a time.** Never list, ticket or place the next group before the current group is placed AND verified (or the user says to skip it). Never combine groups in one confirmation.
- One explicit confirmation per group, for the exact batch shown. Any change (stock, quantity, price) = show the ticket again and re-confirm. Never act on an implied or earlier "yes".
- Signals flagged by the cross-check (`dhan-live/data/signal_crosscheck.json`, recomputed from complete Yahoo history; dh252 stages) are BLOCKED: the builder lists them and will not ticket them. Never work around it; `--override SYMBOL` exists but only the user may ask for it, naming the stock, after you have shown the reason.
- Never retry a rejected/failed order; never modify or cancel without being asked; never place while a refresh is running.

## Guardrails (hard rules)
- Tickets come ONLY from the builders, which refuse (exit code 2) unless `dhan-live/data/guardrails.json` is current for the same signals. A GLOBAL block (trading halt, stale data, a mass-exit anomaly - 5 or more and 20% of positions flipping red at once is treated as suspect data - NaN, cross-broker import, the F5 price cross-check failing on 25% or more of signals) means nothing may be ordered; per-stock blocks (bad P&L, wrong quantity vs holdings, bought-but-unsettled, oversell ...) list the stock as BLOCKED. Never work around a block: tell the user the id and message and stop. Only the user can fix the cause or switch off `dhan-live/data/trading_halt.json`.
- Show every guardrail WARN the builder prints in each stage's ticket, before asking for the "yes".
- Batch caps (`data/guardrails_config.json`: 40 orders, 25% of the capital base per batch); a bigger batch only when the user, after seeing it, explicitly asks for `--allow-big-batch`.
- Kill switch: if the user says stop/halt, set `"halt": true` in `dhan-live/data/trading_halt.json`; set it back to false only when they say so.
- Re-run `python dhan-live/scripts/guardrails.py` right before building tickets if more than a few hours have passed since the refresh.

## Before the stages (once per run)
1. **Login.** `portfolio_agent_tool` action `funds` (cheap) or `holdings`; if it says not logged in, call the MCP `login` tool, give the user the link and wait.
2. **Entries are TODAY's only (user, 2026-11-09).** The dh252 builder lists an entry only when its 52-week-high day is the latest session; a missed day is a missed signal (`--all-days` shows older ones, only when the user asks).
2b. **Fresh data, after the close.** The signals must be the day's final ones: the backtester's Daily OHLC Update publishes after the close (16:00, 17:00 and 19:00 IST runs), then `/refresh-dhan`. The builders refuse data older than 3 days, but you must also check `market_data_date` is the session that just ended.
2c. **Refresh the pending orders.** `orderbook_agent_tool` action `list`; rewrite `dhan-live/data/orders_snapshot.json` with the orders still PENDING (`fetched_at`, `source`, `orders[]` with `order_id, symbol, side, qty, order_type, price (null for market), status, amo`) so the builders skip what is already ordered.
3. **Market must be closed.** NSE equity trades Mon-Fri 09:15-15:30 IST (plus announced special sessions). If it is open, stop. (Weekend/holiday, after 15:30 IST, or before 09:15 = continue in AMO mode.)
4. **Overview.** Run `python dhan-live/scripts/build_order_ticket.py all` and `python dhan-live/scripts/build_share_ticket.py exit`, `add` and `entry` once, and tell the user in one line how many rows each of the six groups has and that you will take them one group at a time in the order above (name the signals the builders skipped because they already have a pending order). Groups with 0 rows are named in that line too, but each empty group is still announced and acknowledged in its own turn (see the loop). This is only a count, not a pick list.

## The loop - repeat for each group in order (stages 1 to 6)
**A0. Empty group.** If the builder shows 0 rows for this group, do NOT silently skip it. Say explicitly, in its own message, "No <exit signals / add-on signals / entry signals / investment-share exits / investment-share averaging / investment-share entries> today for this group" (and, if it applies, why in one line: entries hidden by the cross-check, entries whose 52-week-high day is not today, signals skipped because they already have a pending order, or investment-share averaging blocked by the 7-day cooldown), then ask exactly: "Move ahead to <next group>?" and WAIT for the user's yes before listing the next group. For the last group (investment-share entries) there is no next group: say there are none and that the run is finished. A "skip" or "no" answer ends the run. Never place anything in an empty group.

**A. List.** Re-run the builder for ONLY this group and print the FULL numbered table (numbers restart at 1 in each group), all rows, even if shown earlier (never "same as before"): number, stock, quantity, reference price, est. value, reason. Reasons: exits: P&L %, flip; add-ons: P&L % and days since the last buy; entries: 52-week high and flip; investment-share exits: profit (Rs and %), red since; investment-share averaging: P&L % vs average cost and the cooldown note; investment-share entries: the flip date and the previous capital + profit it is sized from. Print every builder guardrail WARN, `skipped` and BLOCKED line.
- **EXITS (stages 1 and 4):** confirm with `portfolio_agent_tool` `holdings` that each stock's available quantity (`avail`, not T1 or pledged) covers the sale; if it is less, tell the user and drop that stock. For investment-share exits also read the builder's option-leg lines: a `WARNING` means the sale uncovers a short call that is covered today: show it prominently and require the user to acknowledge it in their confirmation (a `NOTE` is information); never touch the option legs. Check `orderbook_agent_tool` list for an open SELL on the same stock (for example a manual order): if one exists, stop and tell the user (the combined quantity could exceed the holding); never cancel it yourself. If a share-exit list is empty, show the red-but-in-loss stocks as "held, no exit" for context.
- **BUY groups (2, 3, 5, 6):** directly under the table show a **capital check**: from `portfolio_agent_tool` action `funds` the Dhan available balance (cash + collateral margin), withdrawable cash, collateral and utilised; for dh252 stages also from `dhan_live_signals.json` the base (Rs20,00,000 fixed), dh252 invested now and the undeployed remainder; this group's est. value (+2% worst case) as a % of available balance, of withdrawable cash (and, for dh252, of the base), and, from the second BUY group on, what the earlier groups already used. Say plainly that most of the available balance is collateral, not cash. Never count exit proceeds as available funds.

**B. Ask, with exclusions.** Ask exactly one question: "Go ahead with all N, or tell me which to exclude (e.g. `exclude 2,4` or a stock name)? Say `skip` to skip this group." Translate the reply into the pick (all minus the exclusions) and echo the final list back. Never default silently; if the reply is unclear, ask again. An excluded stock is simply not ordered (it stays a signal; say so, and do not re-offer it later in the same run). The user may also name stocks, which must come from this same group's list; refuse any stock not in it.

**C. Ticket.** `python dhan-live/scripts/build_order_ticket.py <exit|addon|entry> --pick <numbers>` or `python dhan-live/scripts/build_share_ticket.py <exit|add|entry> --pick <numbers> [--qty SYMBOL=N]`; read the `TICKETS_JSON=` line and the printed total (investment-share exit tickets carry `avg_cost`, `capital`, `profit_est`).

**D. Prices and funds.** For each ticket get the NSE EQUITY `securityId` with `search_agent_tool` action `search`, payload `{"query": "<SYMBOL>-EQ", "exchange": "NSE"}` (the `NSE E ... EQUITY` row; the BSE row is not used; also in `dhan_security_ids.json` / `cc_config.json`), then ONE `market_data_agent_tool` action `ohlc` call `{"NSE_EQ": [<ids>]}` (after the close it returns the finished session as `O/H/L/C`). **If the quote API is down** (found 2026-11-16: `ohlc` and `ltp` returned "Bad Gateway" three times), use `historical_data_agent_tool` action `historical` for the signal day (`{"securityId","exchangeSegment":"NSE_EQ","instrument":"EQUITY","expiryCode":0,"oi":false,"fromDate":<3 days back>,"toDate":<tomorrow>}`): its last candle is the finished session's O/H/L/C; say that this was the source. If a close differs from the ticket's `ref_price` by more than 3%: for BUYs stop and ask; for exits show it as a warning only (every exit signal is exited).
- **BUYs:** limit = min(session high, close x 1.02), the cap rounded to the nearest whole rupee (below), not to the stock's own tick. **Limit rounding (user, 2026-11-26): round every BUY limit to the NEAREST whole rupee (decimal .50 or more rounds up, below .50 rounds down).** After limit = min(session high, close x 1.02), round the result to the nearest whole rupee (DIXON: cap 12,972.36 -> 12,972; its 13,237.55 was rejected 'not a multiple of the tick size'). A whole rupee is valid on every tick up to Rs1 and may sit up to Rs0.49 above the cap, which the user accepts. Exception: a stock with a Rs5 tick (POWERINDIA) rejects a whole rupee that is not a multiple of 5; if a rejection names the tick size, round to a multiple of 5 and re-confirm. Show close, high, cap and limit side by side and mark rows where the cap applied; say plainly that a limit order fills at the open when the stock opens at or below it (you normally pay the opening price), on a gap-up it can fill up to the limit, and above the limit it does not fill. For a investment-share entry the quantity is fixed by the rule; the limit only caps the price. The user may override a price. Funds: `margin_agent_tool` action `single` (NOT `multi`, which returned 0) per order, payload `{"exchangeSegment":"NSE_EQ","transactionType":"BUY","quantity":<q>,"productType":"CNC","securityId":"<id>","price":<limit>}`; compare Total Margin with Available Balance for the group (its "Insufficient Balance" line is nonsense - ignore it). A batch of many small orders may be checked on the largest order plus the full-value sum. Stop and tell the user if funds are short.
- **EXITS (stages 1 and 4):** MARKET, no price: say plainly that the fill is whatever the market gives at the open, which can be well away from the close on a gap-down (for a investment-share exit, a gap-down could turn the profit into a loss).

**E. Confirm.** Show the ticket table for this group (exits: symbol, SELL, quantity, MARKET AMO, est. proceeds at the close, reason, P&L %, and for investment-share exits the capital (qty x average cost), the estimated profit it books and any short-call WARNING; BUYs: symbol, quantity, limit, value at the limit, reason), the total next to available balance / withdrawable cash (and the dh252 base for stages 2-3), every guardrail WARN, and the line "AFTER-MARKET orders for the next trading day". For investment-share exits add: "the position will be fully closed at the opening price". Ask the user to reply "yes, place these" and wait. Nothing is placed before that.

**F0. Investment-share exits only: record the capital BEFORE placing.** For each stage-4 ticket run `python dhan-live/scripts/invest_trades.py pending --symbol <SYM> --qty <qty> --avg-cost <avg_cost> --date <today>` with the values in the ticket (never typed by hand): it captures the position's capital while the holdings snapshot still has it. If an order is rejected, remove or correct that pending record (`invest_trades.py` keeps only the latest pending per stock) or tell the user.

**F. Place** one at a time with `trading_agent_tool` action `place`:
- EXIT (stages 1 and 4): `{"exchangeSegment":"NSE_EQ","transactionType":"SELL","quantity":<q>,"productType":"CNC","securityId":"<id>","price":0,"orderType":"MARKET","validity":"DAY","afterMarketOrder":true,"amoTime":"OPEN"}`.
- ADD-ON / AVERAGING / ENTRY (stages 2, 3, 5, 6): `{"exchangeSegment":"NSE_EQ","transactionType":"BUY","quantity":<q>,"productType":"CNC","securityId":"<id>","price":<limit>,"orderType":"LIMIT","validity":"DAY","afterMarketOrder":true,"amoTime":"OPEN"}` (Dhan's AMO options are PRE_OPEN / OPEN / OPEN_30 / OPEN_60; OPEN unless the user says otherwise).
The tool's own pre-trade funds check is BROKEN (it rejects with a sign-flipped "shortfall" even when the balance is far above the order, and it can block a SELL that needs no margin). **Standing rule (user, 2026-11-18, "yes, override and remember this rule"):** if `place` is blocked as "insufficient funds" AND the margin check from step D shows the funds are fine (the block's own "Available" is far above the order value, its "Shortfall" is just the available balance, or available minus the order value, with the sign flipped; for a SELL the required margin is 0), that is the known fault: do NOT ask again. Retry that order with `"preflight": false`, and use it for the rest of the same group, but only AFTER the user's "yes, place these" for that group (the override is not a new decision). Say in one line that the known fault fired and the override was applied. Place ONE first and verify it as usual. Any OTHER error, or a block where the margin check shows a REAL shortfall (required margin above the available balance), still stops the run and is shown verbatim. On any other validation error STOP and report it verbatim; do not guess other fields. Place ONE first, check it with `orderbook_agent_tool` action `list` (PENDING, no error), then place the rest of the group. Stop at the first rejection, say which orders did and did not go through, and do NOT start the next group until the user says how to proceed. Never retry.

**Verify stops in BOTH books (user, 2026-11-26).** Whenever you check whether an ND short option has a stop or target, or whether the user's manually placed orders are in, read BOTH `orderbook_agent_tool` `list` (regular orders) AND `super_list` (super orders, a separate book: a stop and target placed as a super order never appears in `list`), and say which book you checked. Never report 'not in the book' from `list` alone (found 2026-11-26: the HCLTECH 1280PE super-order stop at 21.45 / target at 2.85 was missed that way).

**G. Verify (this is the gate to the next group).** `orderbook_agent_tool` action `list`: for EVERY order of this group confirm it is present with the expected side, quantity, price and a PENDING (accepted) status, not rejected or cancelled. Report order id and status per stock in a table. Nothing has filled yet; never say it has. Rewrite `dhan-live/data/orders_snapshot.json` with the orders still PENDING (same fields as step 2c), keeping any other pending orders already in it. Then say plainly "<group> done and verified: N of N accepted" and only THEN go to the next group (back to A0/A). If anything is missing or rejected, stop and report; do not continue.

## After the last group
Summarise per group: placed / verified / skipped / excluded / failed (verbatim error). Remind the user: the Dhan tradebook is TODAY-ONLY, so on the next trading day (after the open) `/refresh-dhan` must run: its tradebook step records the fills with `append_fills.py` (quantities come from the order book, since the tradebook reports 0), records share-averaging BUYs in `cc_adds.json` (the weekly cooldown), and a filled dh252 exit becomes a closed trade. For a filled SHARE exit the same refresh then books the profit with `python dhan-live/scripts/invest_trades.py fill --symbol <SYM> --qty <qty> --price <average fill price> --date <date>` (this is what sizes the next entry); if the order did not fill, do not record a fill (the pending record stays until it does or is cleared). Orders that did not fill (limit not reached) stay out of the trade log; say so. A missed day loses the fills.

## Reporting
Plain: placed (as AMO) / status / still pending / failed (verbatim error). Never call a pending AMO filled.
