—
Plain English: For every 1% your portfolio dropped at its worst, how many % did you earn per year?

Above 3 = professional grade. Above 5 = world-class.

₹ Risk per Trade Distribution
Are you consistently risking ~₹1,000 or is your sizing drifting?
What to look for & action ▸
Your rule is ₹1,000 risk per trade. The bulk of bars should sit in ₹500–1.5K.
Oversized (>₹2K): you broke your own rule — likely on a high-conviction trade. Fine occasionally, dangerous as a habit.
Undersized (<₹500): lost conviction after losses? Sizing down when fearful then missing recoveries is a common trap.
Winner vs Loser Hold Duration
Are you holding winners longer than losers? — the key asymmetry check
What to look for & action ▸
In a momentum system, losers should exit fast (stop triggered), winners should run long. You want a big gap between the two bars.
If winners and losers have similar hold times: you're either exiting winners too early or holding losers too long — both hurt the system.
If losers are held longer than winners: serious discipline issue — you're letting losses run and cutting winners short.
Closed Winner Size Distribution
Where do closed winners exit? (open positions excluded — they're your bigger wins)
What to look for & action ▸
Markers show your two key triggers: +5% (move stop to breakeven) and +10% (take 50% partial).
In a trailing-stop system, most closed winners will look small — that's normal. Your big wins are still open, trailing via the 7-day low.
Watch for: many winners clustered in 0–2% means exits are too early or setups are not running at all.
Loss Size Distribution
How large are closed losses as % of the position?
What to look for & action ▸
Amber line = your average actual loss. Most losses should fall in 0–3% (clean, near stop). A long tail into 5–8% or >8% = stops overrun or not taken — a process failure.
Action: If >10% of losses are in the 5%+ buckets, that's your biggest lever to pull. Compare avg actual loss ₹ vs avg init risk ₹ in the KPI cards above.
Entry Precision — Risk% as Stop Proximity Proxy
O'Neil rule: enter within 5% of pivot. Init Risk% ≤ 5% = tight entry near pivot. Comparing WR and avg P&L by risk tier.

Pattern Analysis
Cut Fast, Run Long?
Red = closed losses · Green = closed wins · Amber = open positions (still running, not final)
How to read this chart ▸
Ideal SST pattern: Red dots cluster bottom-left (small losses, cut quickly). Green/amber stretch up and right (big gains, held long).
Warning signs: Red dots spread far right = holding losses too long. Green dots all cramped left = exiting winners too early.
Amber note: Open positions show their gain so far — not their final outcome. They will move right as they age. These are typically your biggest winners.
New Positions Per Week
Amber = weeks with ≥3 entries · bright = current week
How to read this ▸
The key question is not just how many entries per week, but whether high-entry weeks perform worse. If they do → overtrading. If they don't → you were catching genuine breakout clusters.
Warning: Spike weeks that immediately follow a 3+ loss streak are the highest-risk entries (revenge trading or forcing trades in a bad market).
