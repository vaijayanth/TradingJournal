## F&O ban = exit signal (user, 2026-11-26)
A stock in the NSE F&O ban period (`data/fno_ban.json`, fetched by each refresh) allows only reducing trades, so an open option structure on it cannot be rolled, added to or replaced. Each broker's covered-call engine raises an EXIT alert of type `F&O ban` (one per stock with open legs, shorts and hedge, action "Close the structure while the ban lasts"). It shows on the Covered Call tables, in the `*-option-exit-signal` list (group 7.5), in the "Act now" count and in guardrail P3. A failed ban fetch (`ok: false`) raises nothing. Entry rows for banned stocks stay "do not open".

## Option exit criteria (summary, 2026-11-26)
Raised by each broker's `compute_covered_call.py` (thresholds in `data/cc_config.json` `rules`), listed by `*-option-exit-signal`, most urgent first:
| # | Signal | Fires when | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3x premium | short price >= 3x the sold average | close now |
| 2 | Through hedge | stock past the hedge strike (maximum loss) | close or roll |
| 3 | Beyond strike | stock past the short strike (in the money) | roll, close or hold |
| 4 | Stop problems (ND, AZ scans) | stop fired but the short is open; trigger passed but the stop is pending; short with no stop | fix in the broker app / skill |
| 5 | Expiry risk | in the money within 7 days of expiry | close before expiry |
| 6 | Event close | results or ex-dividend within 15 days | close before it |
| 7 | Exit (colour flipped) | open structure is the opposite of the Supertrend colour (AZ RSI override applies) | close, open what the colour wants |
| 8 | F&O ban | stock with open legs is in the NSE ban period | close the structure |
| 9 | Profit exit | premium decayed 60%+ (price <= 40% of sold) | buy back |
| 10 | Near strike | stock within 2% of the short strike | watch |
| 11 | Leftover hedge | a long leg hedges nothing | sell it or book the profit |
| 12 | Over-hedged (2026-11-26) | long puts exceed the short puts of the same stock and expiry (the extra long qty covers nothing) | sell the extra long qty or confirm it is deliberate |
DI only: `Near 3x stop` WARN at 2.5x sold. Not criteria: IVP (information), news (not read), delta (strike choice only), a loss on its own.

## Over-hedge check (user, 2026-11-26)
`structure_alerts` in each broker's `compute_covered_call.py` raises a WARN `Over-hedged` when the long PE quantity of a stock and expiry is larger than its short PE quantity (the other direction, short larger than long, was already `No hedge` / `Partly hedged`). Plain short calls are skipped (the call side is unhedged by design), but a call SPREAD (a long call exists for the stock and expiry) is checked the same way, both directions (updated 2026-11-26: under-hedged call spread = `Partly hedged`, over-hedged = `Over-hedged`). It shows in the Fix tab, the Trades table issue column and the AZ / ND `*-option-exit-signal` list (AZ group 11, ND group 10 with the hedge gaps). First hit: COLPAL 1620PE short 275 vs 1500PE long 550.

## Distance to the Supertrend flip (user, 2026-11-26)
Each broker's `compute_covered_call.py` computes Supertrend(10,3) from Yahoo daily candles for its option stocks (`_st_distances`, cached 30 minutes in `data/st_distance.json`) and writes `st_colour`, `st_line`, `st_price`, `st_dist_pct` and `st_bar` into every stock of `covered_call_signals.json`. `st_dist_pct` is the % the price can fall to the lower line before a green turns red (or rise to the upper line before a red turns green). The Covered Call tables show it as the `To flip` column (amber with a warning sign within 1.5%), the entry-tier lists print it as `toflip`, and a stock WITH open option legs within `near_flip_pct` (default 1.5) raises a WARN `Near flip` (Exit tab, watch). Why: the colour on a knife-edge can differ between feeds (the HCLTECH case on 26 Nov: the backtester said green, TradingView red, the 19 Nov close was 0.5% from the line). The engine's own colour still comes from the backtester feed (standing rule); this column only shows how close the line is. Yahoo can lag a session for some stocks, so `st_bar` says which bar it used. A failed fetch keeps the old cache and never stops the engine.
