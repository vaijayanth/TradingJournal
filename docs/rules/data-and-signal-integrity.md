- **Cross-check ignores later bars (2026-11-09, user):** `crosscheck_signals.py` (both brokers) drops Yahoo bars dated after the signals' `market_data_date` (an unfinished same-day bar while the market is open) and only flags a missing session between Yahoo's last bar and the signal date (a weekend signal date is fine); dropped dates are listed in `bars_after_signal_date_ignored`. So F5 no longer fires just because the market is open. Note the backtester can stamp its last bar with a non-session date (2026-11-08 was a Sunday with `expected_last_session` 2026-11-06 and 32 stale symbols) whose closes differ from the real Friday close: that IS a real data problem and F5 correctly blocks on it.
