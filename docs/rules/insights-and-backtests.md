# Insights and backtests (rules split out of CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-25)

> Verbatim dated notes, original order. Read this file when the task touches: writing insights.json, quoting backtest numbers. Dated notes here still override older ones; CLAUDE.md core rules win on conflicts.

- **Strategy insights box (user, 2026-11-18; ND and AZ).** One reasoned summary box per account on the dashboard (`#d2-insights`, `d2RenderInsights()`, files `zerodha-live/insights.json` and `dhan-live/insights.json`, urls in `WORKSPACES[id].insightsUrl`). It is written by Claude (not by page code) at every `/refresh-zerodha` and `/refresh-dhan` (step 7d / 6f of the skills): headline, 4 to 6 reasoned points, one next action, a confidence level and a maturity line. `insights_log.json` in each folder keeps what was recommended each day, so later insights can say whether earlier advice played out. Rules: reason rather than list, options P&L and rule-breaking leaks first, no rule-change advice below 90 days or 30 closed trades, honest confidence that rises as data grows. No commentary goes on any other card.
- **Insights are written as the best swing trader (user, 2026-11-18).** Every refresh the insights box must be reasoned as a veteran swing trader advising the owner of this book: run the seven lenses in the refresh skills (market breadth, process adherence, book health, risk to exit and options tail, edge vs backtest, opportunity cost, one decision for tomorrow), say "do nothing" when that is right, challenge the owner with evidence when needed, never fiddle with a rule on a handful of trades, and propose rule changes only with backtest evidence from `swing-backtest/`.

- **Backtest summary to remember (user, 2026-11-18; full tables in `swing-backtest/RESULTS.md`).** 10 years (Nov 2016 to Nov 2026), 6% on idle cash, live rules. **ND (Dhan 252-day-high), Rs20L, Rs1k + Rs1k ladder:** CAGR 28.0%, max DD -15.4%, return/DD 1.82, 7,898 trades, win 41.3%, avg win/loss +33.1% / -9.0%, PF 2.01, final Rs2.38 Cr. **AZ (Zerodha F&O, flip-today), Rs30L, Rs20k + Rs5k ladder:** CAGR 20.2%, max DD -21.3%, return/DD 0.95, 3,656 trades, win 41.2%, +21.0% / -6.8%, PF 1.75, final Rs1.90 Cr. **Scaling:** at Rs1 Cr scale base AND step together (ND Rs5k + Rs5k = 28.1% / -15.4%; AZ Rs66.7k + Rs16.7k = 20.2% / -21.7%, both reproduce the small-account result); unscaled ladders under-deploy (ND 18.3%, AZ 17.4%). **Other tests, all on one return/risk line:** AZ entry window 0/1/3/5 is noise (flip-today marginally best); step every 50 trades = faster ramp, more return and more drawdown; ND uncapped doubling fails (-35.5% DD, 1,176 trades), cap Rs1L gives 37.2% / -27.9%; Dhan entry on the F&O universe only matches AZ at 3% sizing (20.8% / -23.2%). Verdict: keep both strategies as they are; the step, not the start size, sets return vs risk. Caveats: survivorship bias, dividend-adjusted prices, no liquidity limits, no tax.
- **Insights facts layer (user, 2026-11-26).** The insights box is no longer free prose: `dhan-live/scripts/compute_insights_facts.py` and `zerodha-live/scripts/compute_insights_facts.py` compute the numbers (see the CLAUDE.md note and the refresh skills, steps 5f / 6f), `verify_insights.py` checks the text, and the six fixed labels are Signals, Options process, Risk, Strategy health, Regime and anomalies, Edge test. Ranked by the research report: (1) rule-adherence ledger, (2) signal-to-fill leakage, (3) live-vs-backtest bootstrap percentile against a matched pool (backtest trades that also closed within the live window, because a young book has only closed its early losers), (4) open risk to the Supertrend line, (5) option-book stress grid, then giveback, trend supply, structure vs colour, add-on contribution (tested: no edge on ND), edge-decay alarm and cost-adjusted premium. Known limits: ND fills are matched from the fills ledger (a 'missed' signal can be a day the refresh did not run), AZ keeps no per-fill ledger (entry slippage unavailable), the option ledger accumulates from 2026-11-26, the stress grid assumes beta 1, and the verifier matches numbers by displayed precision against all fact values (it catches invented numbers, not a real number used for the wrong thing).

- **Insights follow the user's rules (user, 2026-11-26).** Each `compute_insights_facts.py` now writes a `rules_in_force` block read from that broker's `data/rules.json` (add-on switch-off date `add_on_off_from` = 2026-11-30, whether add-ons are active on the data date, days until the switch, the add-on / exit / sizing / entry-window rules, and `swing_add_on_deviations`: any BUY on an already-open swing stock on or after the off date). `edge_tests.add_on_rule_status` states the switch. The skills tell the narrator to judge against this block only and never to suggest averaging a swing loser once add-ons are off.

- **Four-section insights box (user, 2026-11-26, supersedes the six fixed labels above).** `points` = Swing, Options, Events, Risk management, in that order. The facts file gained `swing` (month stats, month-vs-earlier direction, today's signals / fills / realised P&L, open book, robustness with the Nifty vs 50 / 200 day regime and the matched-bootstrap percentile), `options_today` (today's closed contracts, India VIX and its one-year percentile, IVP of the stocks and the universe, premium-rich stocks without a position, favourites / laggards from `option_ledger.json`, which now stores `ivp` and `vix` at close), `events` (results / ex-dividend within 21 days, open legs expiring after an event, month season and 90-day calendar from `option_events.json`) and `risk_daily` (six code-counted discipline checks, guardrails, swing and option risk). Market data (Nifty, VIX via Yahoo) is cut at the signals' market data date. Evidence tiers and the 30-trade rule are unchanged.

- **Close reasons are inferred by code (user, 2026-11-26: 'track close reason, no manual input').** `classify_closes` in each `compute_insights_facts.py` tags every closed option contract with side (short / long, from the day's buy / sell activity, or for ND from the earlier committed `cc_raw/positions.txt`, else spread geometry), buy-back vs sold price, and a reason: profit target (bought back at 44% of the sold price or less), 3x stop (2.7x or more), trend flip (Supertrend colour against the structure), early profit exit, loss exit outside the rules, hedge closed. Stored in `option_ledger.json` from 2026-11-26 and summarised in `options_today.exit_reasons` (counts, P&L, `stop_fired_total`). The 3x stop is the third risk layer (trade with the trend, exit on the flip, 3x for gaps), so a firing stop is the exception to report.
- **Insights voice (user, 2026-11-26).** Conversational English, short sentences, conclusion first, at most 3 or 4 rounded numbers per point; the facts layer still holds every exact figure and the verifier still rejects any number that is not in it.
- **Signal-history cache (user, 2026-11-26).** `git_signal_history()` in each `compute_insights_facts.py` keeps `data/signal_history_cache.json` (commit -> market date, plus a compact entries / exits / add-ons list per date) and runs `git show` only on commits it has not seen, so the run time stays flat as the history grows (it was one `git show` per commit per run, an estimated 10 to 30 seconds a year from now).
